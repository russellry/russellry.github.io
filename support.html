How to Reach Me

For any questions, issues, or feedback regarding my apps, please email me directly at:

russellongdev@gmail.com

I usually respond when I can. Clear reproduction details make support much faster.

Before You Email

To help me assist you as quickly as possible, please include where relevant:

  • App name and version from the app settings or App Store listing.
  • Device model and iOS version.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots or screen recordings are very helpful.
  • Your App Store purchase receipt for billing or refund questions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Bug Reports

Send the steps that caused the issue and I will aim to patch reproducible bugs in a future update.

Billing and Subscriptions

All payments are handled securely by Apple. For refunds, start with Apple's refund request page. If you get stuck, email me and I will guide you.

Privacy Questions

See the Privacy Policy. If something still feels unclear, drop me a line.

Feature Requests

Tell me why the feature matters to you: the use case, the pain point, and what a good outcome would look like.

Quick Troubleshooting

  • Force-quit and relaunch the app.
  • Ensure you are on the latest version from the App Store.
  • Restart your device to clear cached issues.
  • Still broken? Email me with the details above.
